# Product Sales Data Analysis

## Overview
This repository contains a Power BI dashboard designed for analyzing product sales and performance data. The dashboard provides a high-level view of key performance indicators (KPIs) alongside detailed breakdowns of profits, product performance, and regional sales trends.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Units Sold Performance:** 100.06K (Goal: 52.97K, +88.9%) 
* **Sales Performance:** 11.73M (Goal: 5.37M, +118.42%) 
* **Average Discounts Performance:** 9891 (Goal: 6.57K, +50.54%) 
* **Profit Performance:** 2.03M (Goal: 13.01M, -84.44%) 

## Dashboard Insights & Visualizations

### 1. Profit Trend Analysis
* **Visual Type:** Line Chart
* **Description:** Illustrates the continuous upward trajectory of profits across the years 2013 and 2014.

### 2. Country-Wise & Global Distribution
* **Visual Types:** Button Slicers, Map Visual, and Treemap
* **Description:** 
  * Highlights sales and profit distributions across key regions: **Canada, Germany, United States of America, France, and Mexico**.
  * The map provides a geographical representation of global profit distribution, primarily concentrated in North America and Europe.
  * A detailed treemap breaks down Country-Wise Performance by business segments (e.g., Government, Small Business, Enterprise).

### 3. Product Performance Analysis
* **Visual Type:** Column Chart
* **Description:** Ranks products based on overall sales volume. The top-performing products include **Paseo, VTT, and Velo**, followed by Amarilla, Montana, and Carretera.

### 4. Regional Profit Analysis
* **Visual Type:** Column Chart
* **Description:** Compares total profit across the five primary countries, showing a relatively balanced profit generation with slight variations between Canada, France, Germany, Mexico, and the US.
